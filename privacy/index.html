Hardcore Defuse (“the game”) is a mobile game made by 5hz. This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it on iPhone, iPad or Android. In short: the game keeps your progress on your device, has no accounts and no server of its own, and does not sell or share your personal data.

Data the game keeps on your device

Your progress — rounds cleared, stars, best times, coins, badges, items, skins, achievements, settings and the call sign you choose — is saved only in the game’s storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. Exception: if you sign in to Game Center or Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Apple or Google account (see “Game Center and Google Play Games”).

The call sign is any name you type; it is shown only on your own device. You do not need an e-mail address, a phone number or an account to play.

Leaderboards and duels work on your device. A duel code contains only a round number and a time; it is sent to a friend only if you share it yourself.

Game Center and Google Play Games

Signing in is optional. On iPhone the game uses Apple’s Game Center, on Android Google Play Games. While you are signed in, the game sends your results (points, the longest endless run, the daily streak) to their leaderboards, reports the achievements you unlock, and keeps a copy of your progress in your Apple (iCloud) or Google account, so it can be restored on another device. Your Game Center or Play Games name and your leaderboard results are visible to other players as set in those services.

This data goes to Apple or Google, not to us, and their privacy policies apply. You can sign out, hide your profile or delete this data in the Game Center or Google Play Games settings.

Data we collect

The game has no account and never asks for your name, e-mail or phone number. To find crashes and to see how the game is played, the phone apps use Google’s Firebase services:

• Firebase Analytics — usage events (for example: a round started or finished with its result and time, a shop tab opened, coins earned or spent, an ad watched for a reward, an in-app purchase), the device model, system and app version, language and approximate country, and a random app-instance identifier. We use it only for statistics about the game, not to identify you, and we do not combine it with other data.

• Firebase Crashlytics — when the game crashes or hits an error: the error details, the device model, system and app version, and a random installation identifier.

• Firebase Remote Config — to change game settings (for example how often ads appear, or rewards) without an app update and to compare a few variants across groups of players: the app downloads these settings, sending a random installation identifier, the system and app version, language and approximate country.

You can turn all of this off at any time in the game (the game then uses its built-in settings): Settings › “Anonymous statistics and crash reports”. In the EU/EEA, the UK and Switzerland nothing is stored for analytics until you have answered the consent form, and this data is never used for advertising there. Firebase is provided by Google; Google’s privacy policy applies (policies.google.com/privacy). The game does not access your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Advertising

Watching an ad is always optional (for example, to get an extra life after an explosion). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show an ad and measure it, Google may collect information such as device identifiers (including the advertising identifier, if you allow it), your approximate location derived from your IP address, and information about the ads you see and tap. This is described in Google’s policies: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

On iPhone and iPad the game asks your permission before an advertising identifier can be used to show personalised ads (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you can still watch ads; they are not personalised. You can change this at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in the game’s settings.

If you buy “No ads”, the game no longer shows ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The game receives only a confirmation of what was bought; it never sees your name, payment card or billing details. Apple’s and Google’s privacy policies apply to the payment.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device (for example, when your lives are full again or a new daily bomb is ready). They are created on the device itself and are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the game’s settings or in your device settings.

News from us (for example about a new campaign or a weekend sale) is sent through Firebase Cloud Messaging only if you turn on Settings › “News & offers” (it is off until you do). Then your device gets a random push token and the app subscribes it to two groups: all players and your language. The token does not tell us who you are. Turning the switch off unsubscribes the device from both groups; you can also block notifications in your device settings.

Sharing

When you tap “Share”, the game opens your device’s standard share sheet with a short text (your result or a duel code) and a link to the game. Nothing is sent until you choose an app and send it yourself.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your progress stays on your device until you delete the game. Deleting the game deletes all of its data; because we do not have a copy, it cannot be restored and there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Firebase keeps analytics data for at most 14 months and crash reports for at most 90 days; they are linked only to random identifiers. Turning the switch off stops anything new from being sent; to have the existing data deleted, write to us.

To reset ad tracking, reset or limit the advertising identifier in your device settings. Information collected by Google for advertising is handled under Google’s policy, which also explains how to access or delete it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/EEA and the UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to its use, and to withdraw consent. We keep no account data; the statistics and crash reports described above are linked only to random identifiers, and you can stop them in the settings. Advertising data is managed as described above. You can also contact us with any question.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date. Important changes will also be mentioned in the game’s release notes.
